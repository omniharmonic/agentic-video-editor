    Keyboard

    These work when you are not typing in a field. Everything here also has a button.

    Calendar and grid

    Arrow keys
    Move between days in the calendar, or between posts in the grid
    Enter
    Preview the focused post beside the calendar. o opens it in the editor instead
    t
    Jump to today. PageUp and PageDown change the period

    Moving and rescheduling

    m
    Pick up the focused post to move it
    Arrow keys while holding
    One slot at a time; with Shift one day (calendar) or one row (grid)
    Enter while holding
    Drop it here. Esc cancels
    r
    Change time: pick a date and time, or one of its next free slots
    n
    Move it to its next free slot, now
    u
    Move it to Unscheduled
    z
    Undo the last move or reschedule

    Several posts at once

    x
    Select or unselect the focused post. Shift-click does the same
    r or n
    With posts selected, these act on all of them
    Esc
    Close the preview; with nothing open, clear the selection

    Review and editor

    j and k
    Next and previous post in Review
    s
    Send the post back to Claude with a note
    ?
    This list
